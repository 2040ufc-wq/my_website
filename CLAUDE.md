## Design decisions (confirmed with the owner)
- **Font:** Calibri, fallback `Carlito, "Segoe UI", Tahoma, Arial, sans-serif`. Do not introduce other fonts.
- **Style:** dark, professional. Background `#0A0F1C`, alt section `#0E1526`, cards `#111A2E`, borders `#1E2A44`, text `#E8EEF9`, muted `#93A1BD`.
- **Accent:** tech blue `#3B82F6` → `#38BDF8` (gradient for headline highlight; solid accent on competency-card edge).
- **Sections, in order:** Hero + About + stats → Experience (timeline) → Expertise & Projects (5 competency cards + 6 project links) → Contact footer.
- **Site URL:** https://hassanjamel.github.io/ (canonical, OG, sitemap). `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` sit beside `index.html`.
- **Language via URL:** `?lang=en|ar` overrides the saved choice (used for sharing and screenshots).
- **Default language:** Arabic (RTL). Toggle button in the nav; choice stored in `localStorage` (wrapped in try/catch).
- **Responsive:** single breakpoint at 860px; nav links hide, grids collapse to one column.

## Bilingual rules
- Every user-visible string exists twice: `<span class="ar">…</span><span class="en">…</span>`. CSS hides the inactive one via `html[lang]`. Never ship a string in only one language.
- Direction flips with `html[dir]`; use logical CSS properties (`inset-inline-start`, `padding-inline-start`, `margin-inline`) instead of left/right.
- Directional icons (arrows) are mirrored in RTL.
- Emails, phone numbers and Latin names stay LTR (`direction:ltr; unicode-bidi:plaintext`).
- In the SVG generator, Arabic strings get a leading RLM (U+200F) and no `direction` attribute; avoid mixing Latin words mid-sentence in Arabic SVG text (renders out of order).
- Arabic copy: professional Modern Standard Arabic, short, executive tone.
