## 1. Overview
A bilingual (Arabic / English) single-page profile site that repositions Hassan from "Kaggle/data portfolio" to **AI & Digital Transformation Leader** for an executive audience. The page proves business impact first (revenue, scale, leadership) and technical depth second.

## 2. Problem & Opportunity
- The current site reads as a developer portfolio: skill percentage meters, a 69-project list, a "Top 10" heading with fewer than 10 items, no language switch, and a language line that undersells him ("English: limited").
- Hassan is moving from hands-on execution to C-level / digital product governance roles. The site must support that move.
- Opportunity: a focused page that leads with SAR 700M+ digital sales, 20+ years of experience, and AI-agent vision.

## 3. Goals & Non-Goals
**Goals**
1. Communicate executive positioning within 5 seconds (hero headline + 4 key numbers).
2. Fully usable in Arabic (RTL) and English (LTR), with a one-click switch.
3. Dark, professional look in Calibri, matching a Figma design source.
4. Static, fast, free hosting on GitHub Pages; easy for Hassan to edit.

**Non-goals (v1)**
- No CMS, backend, blog, analytics dashboard, or contact form submission.
- No full 69-project catalog on the page (link out to GitHub/Kaggle instead).
- No CV generator.

### 6.2 Non-functional
- **Performance:** single static file, no external requests, no frameworks; target Lighthouse Performance ≥ 95.
- **Responsive:** desktop ≥ 1160px content width; mobile layout at ≤ 860px (single column, nav links hidden).
- **Accessibility:** text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 on dark background, language button labelled, semantic headings, `lang`/`dir` set on `<html>`, keyboard-focusable links.
- **SEO:** title and meta description updated per language; Open Graph, canonical, Person JSON-LD, robots.txt, sitemap.xml (done).
- **Browser support:** current Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox; iOS Safari and Android Chrome.
- **Privacy:** only data Hassan already publishes (email, phone, socials) is shown.
